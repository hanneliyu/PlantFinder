    Plant lists, vegetation zones and areas come from each council. For Inner West, height, sunlight, water, soil and flowering also come from the council list; Hornsby's nursery list gives habitat notes only, so those values are general reference. Ku-ring-gai's lists give soil type, height, shade and damp tolerance, fire-wise plants and (for trees) vegetation community and bush fire suitability, shown under Native to; its other growing details are general reference. North Sydney's native plant list gives sun and soil conditions and its tree guide gives mature heights; vegetation communities come from its Natural Area Survey. Non-local natives (brown) and exotic trees (red) are only from the councils' tree lists. Width, toxicity, allergy and pot size (marked ref) are general horticultural guidance — check before specifying. Plant usage is design guidance; "Fire retardant" means lower flammability, and no plant is fireproof. Photos come from iNaturalist, with Wikimedia Commons as a backup, and are credited to each photographer under their Creative Commons licence. Only openly licensed photos are shown. Widths, frost tolerance and wildlife were cross-checked against Gardening with Angus (facts only; their text and photos are not reproduced). Toxicity and allergy notes are not medical advice. If a child or pet eats a plant, call the Poisons Information Centre on 13 11 26 or your vet.
